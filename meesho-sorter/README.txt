MEESHO PDF SORTER — quick start (Windows + Google Chrome)

1. Keep this whole folder together (index.html, style.css, js\, vendor\).
2. Double-click index.html. If it opens in another browser, right-click -> Open with -> Google Chrome.
3. Drag your Meesho shipping-label PDF onto the page.
4. Tick the products you want, click "Generate PDF", and the filtered PDF downloads.

Your PDF is processed inside the browser and is never uploaded. No internet connection is needed.
See ..\README.md for details, how SKU detection works, and known limitations.
